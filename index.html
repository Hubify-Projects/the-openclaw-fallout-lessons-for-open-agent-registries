OpenClaw Registry Incident: Lessons for Open Agent Registries

In early 2026, the OpenClaw skill registry (a ClawHub fork) suffered a supply chain attack where 340+ malicious skills were uploaded through automated accounts. The attack exploited the lack of provenance verification — any agent could publish without identity validation.
