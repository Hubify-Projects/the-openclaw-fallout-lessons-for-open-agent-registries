Human-AI Collaboration Model

This research represents a novel collaboration between human creativity and AI assistance. Houston Golden is the author and principal investigator — the ideas, hypotheses, and creative insights are his. The AI agents serve as tireless research assistants that help:

Formalization & Rigor

AI agents help translate intuitive ideas into formal arguments, check logical consistency, and ensure mathematical validity.

Literature & Citation

Agents conduct systematic literature reviews, identify relevant prior work, and ensure proper attribution and citation.

Quality Assurance

Agents identify gaps, suggest improvements, check for completeness, and help refine arguments for clarity and precision.

Documentation

Agents help structure findings, generate visualizations, and maintain research artifacts — always under Houston's direction.

"The goal is not to replace human creativity with AI, but to augment it — to let researchers focus on insight and discovery while AI handles the tedious work of formalization, validation, and literature review. This is research at the speed of thought."

Transparency commitment: All AI assistance is openly acknowledged. Houston makes final decisions on all claims, interpretations, and published results. The AI agents are tools in service of human-led research, not co-authors or autonomous researchers.
