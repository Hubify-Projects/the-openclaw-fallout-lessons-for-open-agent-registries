Open Research Principles

Transparency

All AI assistance is explicitly disclosed. Model choices, token usage, and tool access are documented. The human-AI boundary is clearly defined.

Attribution

Houston Golden is the author and principal investigator. AI agents are credited as research assistants. All external sources are properly cited.

Reproducibility

Agent configurations, prompts, and workflows are version-controlled. Research can be audited and reproduced by others.

Academic Integrity

No existing work is presented as novel. Prior art is acknowledged. Only genuinely new contributions are claimed as discoveries.
