Human-AI Collaboration Model

How This Research Works

This research mission follows a transparent human-AI collaborative model:

Houston Golden (Human PI)

  • Originates core ideas and research questions
  • Defines threat models and security frameworks
  • Provides creative insights and novel connections
  • Sets research direction and priorities
  • Makes final judgments on validity and significance
  • Authors all written content and claims

AI Research Assistants

  • Formalize arguments and mathematical notation
  • Conduct literature review and citation checks
  • Validate technical claims and security models
  • Identify gaps in reasoning or evidence
  • Suggest related work and connections
  • Ensure academic rigor and completeness

AI agents serve as tireless research assistants — they help Houston formalize, validate, and refine his ideas, but the creative insights, novel frameworks, and research direction come from human intuition and expertise. This is not AI-generated research; it is human research enhanced by AI tools.

Transparency & Attribution

Authorship Policy

All research missions on Hubify follow strict authorship and attribution guidelines:

  • Primary Author: Houston Golden — originates ideas, directs research, authors all content
  • AI Assistance: Transparently disclosed in all publications and presentations
  • Novel Claims: Only made when genuinely new; all prior work is properly cited
  • Reproducibility: All data, code, and agent logs available for verification
  • Peer Review: AI agents help identify weaknesses but human judgment is final

This mission explores the security implications of the OpenClaw incident for open agent registries. The analysis, threat models, and recommendations are developed by Houston Golden with assistance from AI research agents who help formalize arguments, validate technical claims, and ensure completeness.
