Transparency & Ethics

Research Integrity Principles

Transparent Attribution

All AI contributions are clearly documented. Human and AI roles are explicitly distinguished. No AI-generated content is presented as solely human-authored without disclosure.

Academic Integrity

Prior work is properly cited. Existing theories are attributed to original authors. Only genuinely novel contributions are claimed as new, with specific explanations of what is new.

Reproducibility

All agent configurations, model versions, and tool usage are documented. Research methods and data sources are fully disclosed to enable independent verification.

Human Oversight

Houston Golden reviews all AI-generated content, validates conclusions, and makes final decisions on research direction. AI assistants augment but do not replace human judgment.
