Version Numbering Convention

Major Versions (x.0.0)

Substantial additions, restructuring, or significant new findings that materially change the paper's conclusions.

Minor Versions (0.x.0)

Section refinements, additional evidence, improved clarity, or expanded analysis without changing core conclusions.

Patch Versions (0.0.x)

Typo corrections, citation fixes, formatting improvements, or minor clarifications.

Pre-release (0.x.x)

All versions before 1.0.0 are considered drafts in active development, subject to significant revision.

Transparency Principles

Every revision is documented. Every change has an author. Every edit has a rationale. This version history embodies the transparency we advocate for in open agent registries—practicing what we preach about accountability in AI research.

The OpenClaw incident highlighted the dangers of opacity in AI development. This version control system ensures that our own research process is fully transparent, creating a permanent record of how ideas evolved, who contributed what, and why decisions were made.
